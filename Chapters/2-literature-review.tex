\section{Citations}

Use \verb|\citet{key}| for a narrative citation, as in \citet{page2021}, and \verb|\citep{key}| for a parenthetical one \citep{lamport1994}. Several works: \citep{lamport1994,page2021}. With a page: \citep[p.~5]{page2021}.

\section{Tables and figures}

Table~\ref{tab:example} shows a table with ragged-right cells that fit the text width.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{An example table.}\label{tab:example}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.5cm}L}
\toprule
Column one & Column two \\
\midrule
Item & A longer description that wraps onto a second line when it reaches the edge of the column. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Figures are placed in the \texttt{Figures} folder and included inside a \texttt{figure} environment with a caption and a label:
\begin{verbatim}
\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{name}
  \caption{Caption text.}\label{fig:name}
\end{figure}
\end{verbatim}
